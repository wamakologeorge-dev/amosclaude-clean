# Amosclaud community support policy

Amosclaud accepts voluntary contributions through the official Cash App destination:

- `https://cash.app/$kenjamakulu`

## Contribution-based service access

A developer must contribute before contribution-funded service capacity can be assigned. The Cash App link does not provide automatic payment verification to Amosclaud.

The controlled process is:

1. The developer sends a contribution through the official Cash App link.
2. The developer includes a recognizable Amosclaud account email or GitHub username in the payment note.
3. Amosclaud verifies the transfer.
4. Amosclaud records the approved amount, duration, features, and usage limits for the developer account.
5. Service pauses after the approved allocation is exhausted.

A screenshot, transaction reference, or payment-link visit does not independently activate service. Reversed, disputed, fraudulent, or unverifiable transfers do not qualify.

## Non-refundable policy

Contributions are voluntary and non-refundable after they are sent, except where a refund is required by applicable law. Contributors should not send an amount they cannot afford to lose.

Contribution-funded service remains subject to:

- platform and model availability;
- repository authorization;
- safety and acceptable-use controls;
- the limits recorded for the developer account;
- Cash App transfer availability and account rules.

A contribution does not guarantee uninterrupted service, a specific software result, a repository repair, a deployment, or a model response.

## Organization and tax notice

The support page describes Amosclaud as an open-source community initiative. It does not claim that Amosclaud is a legally registered tax-exempt charity.

Contributions are not represented as tax-deductible unless the contributor receives an official receipt that explicitly states otherwise. Legal nonprofit or charitable status must be supported by the appropriate registration and documentation before Amosclaud presents itself that way.

## Security

Contributors must never place passwords, API keys, private keys, recovery codes, or other secrets in a Cash App payment note. Amosclaud will never request a Cash App password, PIN, sign-in code, or recovery information.
