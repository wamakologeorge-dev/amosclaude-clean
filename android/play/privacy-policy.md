## 1. Who we are

Amosclaud ("Amosclaud," "we," "us") operates the AI engineering platform at https://amosclauds.com and the Amosclaud Android application (the "App"). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the native Android client and the platform it connects to.

## 2. Information we collect

The App sends the following information to the Amosclaud platform when you use its features:

- **Account information:** name, email address, password or other authentication information you provide to register or sign in.
- **Account and platform content:** repository metadata, chat prompts and assistant responses, community posts/feed content, mail messages, pipeline/deployment inputs, and data returned for platform, storage, or administrator views that you request through the App.
- **Session information:** authentication session cookies received from the service. The App stores these locally in Android SharedPreferences to keep you signed in.
- **Connection preference:** the API server URL you choose is stored locally in Android SharedPreferences.

The App does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, or storage permissions. It contains no advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics SDKs.

## 3. How we use information

We use information to create and authenticate accounts; provide repository, platform, administrator, and AI-chat features; maintain your authenticated session; secure the service; respond to support requests; and operate and improve Amosclaud.

## 4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information or share App data with third parties for advertising. Data is sent to the Amosclaud service at amosclauds.com to provide requested features. We may use service providers to operate the platform under appropriate obligations, or disclose information where required by law.

## 5. Security and retention

The production service is accessed over HTTPS, which encrypts data in transit. Session cookies and the selected API URL are stored locally in Android SharedPreferences; Android backups are disabled for the App. We retain account and platform data while needed to provide the service, subject to legal obligations and our finalized retention practices.

## 6. Your choices and account deletion

You can delete your account in the App. You may also use the web account-deletion page at **https://amosclauds.com/account/delete** once it is available, or contact **[FILL IN: privacy/support email]**. Account deletion removes the account and associated data subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

## 7. Children

Amosclaud is not directed to children and is not intended for users under 13 (or the minimum age required in their jurisdiction).
