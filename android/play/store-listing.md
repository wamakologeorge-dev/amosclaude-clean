## Full description (4,000 characters max)
Amosclaud is a mobile client for the Amosclaud engineering platform. Sign in to stay connected to your repositories, automation work, and AI-assisted engineering tasks while away from your desk.

Use the app to review repositories and their status, follow platform activity, and start or continue an AI chat session. Ask the Amosclaud assistant about an engineering task, then review its responses and session progress from your phone. The app also provides an admin overview for authorized administrators, including platform counts and status.

### Work with your platform account
- Sign in or create an Amosclaud account.
- View repositories connected to your account.
- Send messages to the Amosclaud AI assistant and continue a chat session.
- Review platform and pipeline-related status available in your account.
- Access the administrator overview when your account has administrator access.
- Configure the server URL and test the connection when permitted by your organization.

Amosclaud is designed for software engineers and teams using the Amosclaud platform. An Amosclaud account and internet connection are required. Features and access depend on your account permissions and the services enabled by your organization.
