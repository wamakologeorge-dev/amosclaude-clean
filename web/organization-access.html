Identity and recovery rules

      
    
        
  • Organization IDs contain exactly five numbers, such as 11111.
    • 
        
  • Member IDs combine the organization ID and a four-digit member number, such as 11111-2131.
    • 
        
  • A member ID is public identification, not a password.
    • 
        
  • Temporary access codes are hashed, expire, and have a limited number of uses.
    • 
        
  • Recovery codes are generated randomly, shown once, hashed in storage, and invalidated after use.
    • 
        
  • Removing a member revokes that membership and ends the member's active Amosclaud sessions, but does not expose their password or recovery codes.
    • 
        
  • The final owner must transfer ownership to another active member before leaving the organization.
