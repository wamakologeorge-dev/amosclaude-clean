Contribution-based access

How developer service access works

  1. Contribute first.Open the official Cash App link and send the amount you choose.
  2. Use a recognizable reference.Include your Amosclaud account email or GitHub username in the Cash App note. Never include passwords, API keys, private keys, or recovery codes.
  3. Wait for verification.The Cash App link does not automatically notify or activate Amosclaud. A contribution must be verified before service capacity is assigned.
  4. Receive bounded service capacity.After verification, the account receives the amount, duration, feature limits, and usage allowance shown in its approved service allocation.
  5. Use only the approved allocation.Service pauses when the verified allocation is exhausted. Additional capacity requires another verified contribution.

Transparent activation

No automatic entitlement

Opening Cash App, sending a screenshot, or entering a transaction reference does not by itself activate service. Amosclaud must verify the contribution and record the approved allocation first.

Capacity and availability

Service remains bounded

Contribution-based access is subject to platform availability, safety controls, acceptable-use rules, repository authorization, and the specific limits confirmed for the developer account.

Contribution policy

Non-refundable policy

Contributions are voluntary and non-refundable after they are sent, except where a refund is required by applicable law. Do not contribute an amount you cannot afford to lose.

  • Cash App fees, reversals, disputes, account restrictions, and transfer availability are governed by Cash App.
  • Contributions do not guarantee uninterrupted service, a specific result, repository repair, deployment, or model response.
  • Service benefits begin only after Amosclaud verifies the contribution and confirms the developer's bounded allocation.
  • Fraudulent, reversed, disputed, or unverifiable transfers do not qualify for service access.
  • This page does not claim that Amosclaud is a tax-exempt charity. Contributions are not represented as tax-deductible unless an official receipt explicitly states otherwise.
