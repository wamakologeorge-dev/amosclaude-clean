# License and Ownership

Amosclaud intentionally keeps two ideas beside each other.

## The MIT wall

The Amosclaud repository root keeps its MIT license for material that is not expressly assigned another license. The MIT side is the open wall: developers can understand, use, modify and build with the material that remains under those terms.

The Book does not erase that wall and does not pretend old permissions never existed.

## The Amosclaud Book home

Amosclaud Book is a separately identified product inside the larger Amosclaud repository. New Book material that carries `LicenseRef-Amosclaud-Book-Proprietary-1.0` belongs to the Book's proprietary license scope.

The Book remains readable to humans and agents. Reading is not treated as wrongdoing. Normal temporary browser, cache, execution, backup and security-scanning copies needed to use the Book are allowed by the Book license.

What requires Amosclaud permission is taking the Book outside that reading/use relationship: official copy/export, redistribution, mirroring, resale, sublicensing, white-label use, competing-product extraction or separately distributed derivatives.

## Signed provenance

When Amosclaud authorizes an official Book copy or export, Amosclaud can attach a provenance receipt. The receipt records safe facts such as:

- the Book license identifier;
- the licensed Amosclaud account or organization scope;
- an optional repository scope;
- the Book version and content hash;
- the authorized action;
- the issue time; and
- an Ed25519 signature from the Amosclaud Book signing identity.

The receipt does **not** contain Amosclaud private signing keys, API keys, access tokens or passwords.

A changed document will no longer match its original content hash. A forged receipt will not validate against the trusted Amosclaud signer.

## No hidden surveillance

Protecting the Book is not permission to spy on developers. Amosclaud Book licensing must not secretly collect unrelated device activity or credentials. License audit data stays limited to the Amosclaud account, organization, repository, license, Book version, action and provenance information needed to operate the product.

## Blocking before billing

When an identified account or organization has no Book permission for an official copy/export action, Amosclaud should block that action and record a safe audit event.

An invoice is a separate legal and billing action. Amosclaud must not invent a debt for an anonymous copier or charge a customer unless that customer accepted terms that authorize the charge.

## Transition rule

The Book proprietary designation is prospective. It cannot retroactively cancel rights already granted for a particular older copy under MIT or another earlier license. New Book versions make their applicable license explicit so developers are not asked to guess.

## Canonical terms

The complete Book license is `LICENSES/Amosclaud-Book-Proprietary-1.0.txt` and the repository license map is `LICENSE-SCOPE.md`.

The current v1.0 license is a production draft. Amosclaud should obtain software/IP legal review before relying on it for material commercial enforcement or cross-jurisdiction disputes.
